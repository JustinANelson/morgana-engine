# Halcyon Reach (`games/halcyon-reach`)

Added 2026-11-15: an original online RPG built on Morgana's ORPG module (`combat.module = "realtime"` plus `parties`, `guilds`, `trading`, `crafting`, `projectiles`, `subTileMovement`, `softSeams`, `exploration`, `friends`, `ignore`, `market`, `mail`, and `rolePlayChat` in `server.json`, with `instancing: false` for open-world zones). All game rules, content, Lua scripts, and art live inside `games/halcyon-reach/`, keeping the base engine and ORPG module decoupled.

## World and Content
- **30 maps across 20 tilesets** (27 overland, dungeon and hall maps plus the Guildhall, Grand Hall and Arena; tilesets `2.png`–`10.png` plus additive `TilesetGen` sheets `11.png` grass-on-dirt autotile, `12.png` 3×2 oak tree, `13.png` cobble-on-grass autotile, `14.png` 1×2 pine tree, `15.png` 3×2 stone archway, `16.png` grass-on-water autotile, `17.png` grass tuft, `18.png` wildflower patch, `19.png` bush, `20.png` tree stump, and `21.png` 4-variant meadow grass, 3-frame calm water, and dirt ground sheet; plus the 2026-11-21 town art: `22.png` large cottage, `23.png` inn, `24.png` smithy, `25.png` shop, `26.png` chapel, `27.png` guardhouse, `28.png` great hall, `29.png` thatched cottage (all `BuildingGenerator`) and `30.png` town paving (pale cobble, warm flagstone, road cobble in 4 variants each); `31.png` plaza props (fountain, well, lamp post, bench, planter) and `32.png` ground-detail decals; 2026-11-21 town sets: `33.png`-`38.png` desert buildings (Saltmere), `39.png`-`44.png` ember buildings (Ashgate Keep), `45.png`-`50.png` port buildings (Skerry Landing), `51.png`-`52.png` red and brown market shops (Harrow Bazaar); `30.png` has a fourth paving row, ember cobble), laid out as a hub-and-spoke ring: Harrow Cross (140x140 hub) with Wrenfall Wood and Wrenfall to the south, the Ashgate Causeway, Ashgate Keep and Ashgate Plain to the east, Greyspine Pass, Stonereach, Gloamstone Crags and the Ancient Mine to the north, Sunscald Road, Saltmere, Glasswaste and Starfall Field to the west, and a ferry from the east docks to Skerry Landing, Skerry Isle, Hollow Deep, Undercroft 1F-4F and Gravewatch; plus the Harrow Bazaar, the Stockade and the Holdfast. Overland maps join with seamless soft-seam borders (80% of an edge closed, a 6 tile choke point at the same coordinate on both sides); caves, dungeons and the Bazaar use portals and events. The new-player start is Wrenfall (map 3). The Guildhall (map 28) is a per-guild instanced hall behind an arch in Harrow Cross (members only; practice yard with dummies, a vault alcove with the Guild Quartermaster and the shared guild bank: members deposit, officers withdraw by default, the founder changes the rank). Towns have cross streets and themed quarters, rivers have 3 tile narrows a hero can jump (jump range 4; `jump.disabledMapIds` lists the Stockade and Holdfast), and the PvP borders have gatehouse towers with Town Guards. ## Mounts and pets
Three mounts (`Plains Pony` Lv 20, `Greyspine Warhorse` Lv 50, `Skerry Skyrunner` Lv 90, items 104 to 106) lengthen the stride from the Mount slot; the Stablemaster (`npc 93`, `shop 13`, Harrow Cross by the Pet Handler) and the Market Supply sell them. Four pets (items 118 to 121) come from the Pet Handler (`npc 92`) and are managed in the Pets window or with `/pet`. Mounts and pets have no sprites of their own yet (pets borrow monster sprites).

## Standing and Battle Power
`game.json` carries `nobility` (six cosmetic donation ranks, `Esquire` 20,000 to `Paramount` 10,000,000 Marks, tracked in the player variable `nobility_donated`; `battlePower: 0` so donations grant prestige rather than combat stats) and `battlePower` gear-score weights. The `Standing Herald` (`npc 62`, `event 11`, Market) converts Marks into donations; the Character window's Stats tab shows Battle Power and rank. Event text supports `{nobility}` and `{bp}`.

## Durability and Repair
`game.json` enables `durability` with per-hit wear off (`weaponWearChancePercent: 0`, `armorWearChancePercent: 0`, repairs cost 20% of item price) and `deathWearPoints: 8` on death. Broken gear stops counting until a `Repair Smith` (`npc 63`, `event 12`; Harrow Cross, Ashgate Keep, Saltmere, Market) repairs it for Marks.

## Promotions
`game.json` enables `promotion` (Novice, then Journeyman at Lv 30, Expert at Lv 60, Paragon at Lv 90). The `Calling Master` (`npc 65`, `event 14`, Harrow Cross) promotes for 3 Starshards, 10 Starshards and 3 Wyrm Pearls. Bladestorm, Hail of Arrows, Soothing Tide (Journeyman), Cinder Fall, Rekindle, Heartseeker, Arrow Rain (Expert) and every Lv 30+ gear piece need a rank (`promotionReq`); each promotion also grants bonus attribute points. **Class titles and skills (2026-11-19)**: each class has its own title for every rank (Duelist Fencer, Blademaster, Bladesaint; Bulwark Shieldbearer, Bastion, Titan; Ranger Scout, Pathfinder, Windwalker; Tidecaller Acolyte, Wavecaller, Tidewarden; Emberweaver Cinder Adept, Pyromancer, Inferno Sage), and promotion to Expert and to Paragon teaches that class one signature skill (spells 41 to 50, no book): Flickering Step and Thousand Cuts, Bastion Stance and Titan's Might (a party buff), Piercing Shot and Windwalk, Tidal Ward and Tide's Return (party shield and heal), Immolate and Inferno Nova.

## Dual-wielding
`game.json` enables `dualWield` for Duelists (`classIds` 1): a second one-handed blade, sword or katana is worn in the off-hand slot, and each landed melee hit has a 65% chance of an extra strike for 80% damage. Bulwarks keep the shield; a worn shield or a two-handed main weapon rules out the off-hand weapon.

## Gems and upgrades
Six gems (Garnet attack, Sunstone spirit, Opal agility, Jasper vitality, Onyx armour, Prism Stone kill XP) go into open sockets. Each also has a **Fine** (Lv 30) and a **Mythic** (Lv 60) cut, items 122 to 133, made at any anvil (recipes 12 to 23): three of the tier below plus Marks (2,000; then 20,000 and a Wyrm Pearl). Bonuses go 5, 12, 25 for Garnet attack and Onyx armour, 3, 7, 15 for Sunstone spirit, Opal agility and Jasper vitality, and 5, 8, 12 percent kill XP for Prism Stone; the cuts reuse the plain gems' icons for now. Plus levels come from merging gear (a +N piece joined with another of the same kind at +N or better becomes +N+1). At the Gear Master, Starshards raise gear to its next level and never fail (3 Starshards). Wyrm Pearls raise its quality and can fail: Fine 60% (1 pearl), Enchanted 40% (1), Exalted 25% (2), Mythic 12% (3). Each failure builds a pity stack worth +4% on the next try (25 stacks makes any step certain) and a success spends them. A successful quality raise has a 5% chance to open a socket (up to 2). Sockets are not sold; the other source is dropped loot (`socketChanceBasisPoints`, see Bestiary and loot).

## Bestiary and loot
Thirty-two creatures in nine regions (Stubblehens in Wrenfall Wood up to the Ebbmother in Gravewatch; table in `games/halcyon-reach/RETHEME.md`, section 7). Marks per kill are `0.5 + 0.4 x level` (bosses six times) and shops pay 30% for loot, tuned by the economy model (`HalcyonEconomyModelTest`). Every creature drops Starshards and Pearl Fragments (item 15); bosses always leave 2 to 4 Starshards and 4 to 8 fragments. Eight fragments and 500 Marks bind into a Wyrm Pearl at the Gemwright's anvil. Dropped gear can carry an empty socket by rarity (`socketChanceBasisPoints`, 1% to 15%). Mountain, glass-desert and deep creatures also drop ore.

## Mining
A `Miner's Pickaxe` (item 77, from the Market Merchant or the Ashgate Warden) lets a hero break the ore veins of Gloamstone Crags (`map 5`): 10 Copper, 7 Iron and 4 Gold veins, drawn with ore tiles in the Gloamstone Crags tileset. Ores (items 78-80) sell for Marks; Iron veins can hold a Jasper and Gold veins a Starshard or a Wyrm Pearl (`bonusDrops`).

## Guild War
`game.json` enables `guildWar`: a 10-minute war every hour in the `Holdfast` (`map 27`, an arena with a wall, two `Holdfast Gate`s and the `Banner Stone`), or on `/guildwar start` from staff. Guilds enter through the `Guild Steward` in Harrow Cross (`npc 68`, next to the Calling Master) and leave through the `Holdfast Steward` (`npc 69`). The guild that deals the most pole damage holds the area and each online member receives 5 Starshards.

## Ghosts and Rekindle
`game.json` enables `ghost` (`autoReleaseTicks` 1200): a hero who dies stays where they fell as a pale ghost who can walk but not fight, cast, use items or trade. A Expert-rank Tidecaller's `Rekindle` (spell 17, 5 tiles, `revivePercent` 70) brings back the ghost you clicked (else the nearest one) on the spot; otherwise `/release` or the timer sends the ghost home with full health. Arenas and PK jail still respawn at once. Dying also costs 3 durability on every worn piece (`deathWearPoints`), and `/revive` (`selfReviveAfterTicks` 200, `selfRevivePercent` 40) lets a ghost get up where they fell after ten seconds. `rested` turns on rested XP (10% of a level's XP per offline hour, cap 150%, doubling kill XP while it lasts) and a +25% kill bonus below level 80.

## Ascension
Since 2026-11-19 `game.json` has no `rebirth`; it uses the non-destructive `paragon` track instead (see [combat](/systems/combat.md)): at Lv 120 the `Ascension Envoy` (`event 4`) offers a free Ascension that teaches `Ascendant Aura` (`spell 19`) and, to Bulwarks, `Reflect` (`spell 20`; neither is sold as a book) and sets the variable `ascended`; XP at the cap then earns Paragon levels (2,000,000 XP for the first, 20,000 more for each next, 1 attribute point each, 100 at most). The XP curve is `30 * level^2` (about 13 hours to Lv 120 in `HalcyonEconomyModelTest`, down from 34). Three bounty clerks (NPCs 77 to 79 in Harrow Cross, Ashgate Keep and Saltmere, events 19 to 21) post six daily quests (18 to 23) that return 1,728,000 ticks (24 hours) after the hand-in. `sprint` (hold `Shift`, 150% step, 1 stamina per 2 ticks) and `townGate` (the `Town Portal Scroll`, `item 103`, sold in town shops; fizzles on the Stockade and Holdfast) are on too.

## Sockets, Gems, and Upgrades
The `Gear Master` (`npc 64`, `event 13`; Harrow Cross and the Market) works worn gear through the `upgrade_gear` step: a Wyrm Pearl opens a socket (max 2), a carried gem is set into it (`Paragon` +5 damage, `Ashgate` +3 SPI, `Rainbow` +5% kill XP, `Moon` +3 AGI, `Violet` +3 STR, `Fury` +3 damage and +1 STR, `Kylin` +3 VIT, `Tortoise` +5 armour), 3 Starshards raise gear to its next tier (70%), and Wyrm Pearls raise its quality (40% for 1, sure for 3). `+` level, sockets and rolls carry over.

## New-player tips
`game.json` has an `onboarding` section that rewrites the built-in tips for this world: a welcome (Wrenfall, the quest log), skills (Skill Books from the Training Master), gear wear and breakage (Repair Smith towns), low health (Pharmacists), death (ghost, `/release`, Rekindle, the one-minute auto-release) and two level tips: promotion at 30 (Calling Master, 3 Starshards) and Ascension at 120. Stat points and equipping use the built-in tips. See [new-player tips](/systems/onboarding.md). Test: `HalcyonReachGameTest`.

## Paperdoll art (TilesetGen)
`game.json` sets a `sprites` profile with `paperdollingV2`: 32 x 48 cells (RMXP size), 4 direction rows and the columns idle 2, walk 4, attack 4, cast 4, harvest 4, jump 3, hurt 2 (736 x 192 px). `uiHeights` lists each sprite's visible height (computed from the art) so name plates, bars and damage text sit on each sprite's head. Since 2026-11-21 it is drawn by `:tilegen` itself: `.\gradlew.bat :tilegen:run "--args=halcyon export --game games/halcyon-reach"` ([`HalcyonGameExporter`](/architecture/tilegen.md)) writes sprites 1-5, 10-21, 101-122 and paperdolls 1-21 in the `MORGANA` style (soft outline, rim shading, muted colour, soft shadow) so they match the map art; the class looks come from `tilegen/specs/halcyon_<class>.json`.
- **Size**: the characters are the taller RMXP proportions (about 23 x 40 px in a 32 x 48 cell), a little taller than the NPCs and enemies (about 30 px); no draw scale is used. `sprites.scales` stays available per sprite id if a class needs enlarging.
- **Class bodies** (sprites 1 to 5: Duelist, Bulwark, Ranger, Tidecaller, Emberweaver): every layer that is not swappable gear (body, eyes, hair, legs, feet, tunic, waist, shoulders) baked in the doll's per-direction order. Headgear, held items, cape, back items, aura and shadow are left out and come from items.
- **Gear overlays** (`paperdolls/1` to `21`, `27`): bows 1, quiver 2, blades 3 (off-hand 4), swords 5 (6), katanas 7 (8), arcane staff 9, pickaxe 10, shields 11 to 13, helmets 14 to 16, armour 17 to 19, boots 20 and 21, amulet 27. The script writes each item's `paperdollSpriteId`, `paperdollOffHandSpriteId` and `paperdollSlot`.
- **Colour**: the overlays are neutral grey-silver parts, so the former `paperdoll.py` sets each item's `paperdollTintHex` to the hue of that item's own icon (when the icon is clearly coloured and the part is not already that colour); the worn Traveler Armor, Brigandine and Skyrender tiers then match their green, purple and bright-green icons. It is recomputed on every run, so re-running after changing icons keeps them in step.
- **Class armour colours**: each class starts in its own armour instead of a shared Traveler Armor: Duelist's Jerkin (#98, crimson), Bulwark Cuirass (#99, steel blue, the plated brigandine overlay), Ranger's Tunic (#100, forest green), Tidecaller's Vestments (#101, sea teal) and Emberweaver's Vestments (#102, ember orange). Each has `classReqIds`, its own icon (`ICON_RECOLORS` with an absolute hue) and a hand-picked worn colour in the former `paperdoll.py`'s `TINT_OVERRIDES` (which wins over the icon-derived tint). Only the starting tier is class-coloured so far; the Brigandine and Skyrender tiers are still shared. Test: `every class starts in its own armor with its own colour`.
- **Class colours on all gear**: every weapon, shield, helmet, armour and boots overlay is worn in the wearer's class colour through the item field `paperdollClassTints` (classId -> `#rrggbb`; the engine picks the wearer's entry, else `paperdollTintHex`): crimson Duelist, steel-blue Bulwark, green Ranger, teal Tidecaller, orange Emberweaver. the former `paperdoll.py` writes them (`CLASS_HUES`, `CLASS_TINT_STYLES`): the colour deepens with the gear tier, metal blades are only lightly tinted and bows and staves sit in between. One shared item therefore looks different per class and the classes stay apart at every tier, with no per-class copies of items, drops, shops or upgrade chains. Test: `worn gear is coloured per class so the classes stay apart at every tier`.
- **Sideways legs**: TilesetGen now draws legs and feet facing left and right, so the exported sprites and `legs_*` / `feet_*` overlays use them. Tidecaller and Emberweaver have no leg or feet layer at all (their robe covers them).
- **Attack**: TilesetGen's `slash` for every body and overlay, `shoot` for the Ranger's body, bows and quiver (a worn piece must use the same action as the body under it). `thrust`, `run`, `sit`, `emote` and `ko` have no engine state yet.
- **NPCs and enemies**: TilesetGen's `npcs/<id>.png` (sprites 10 to 21) and `enemies/<id>.png` (101 to 122) replace the sprite with that id, on the same grid and all nine actions (attack is `slash`). Any older 3-frame Time Fantasy sheet left over (sprite 6) is upgraded in place (attack, cast and harvest repeat the walk, jump holds a walk frame, hurt is the standing frame).
- The script reads each pack PNG on the class sheets' grid (36 rows: one row per action and direction, frames across).
