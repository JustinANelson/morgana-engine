## 2026-11-22

* **Add** - **Hand-pixelled terrain look in tilegen: `TUFTED_GROUND` and autotile edge finishes** ([tilegen](/architecture/tilegen.md)): a first step toward the soft, outlined look of commercial terrain sheets (the Trevana Realms `sample1.png` and `sample2.png`). `MaterialPattern.TUFTED_GROUND` is calm flat ground (coarse posterised patches, scattered lit blades) instead of 1 px fBm static; `AutotileStyle.edge` (`SOFT` is the old look, `OUTLINED`, `LUSH`) draws a hard silhouette with a fill-derived outline, a lit upper-left band, a shaded lower-right band and a background shadow (`shadow`), and `LUSH` adds jutting grass tufts. Both are spec fields (`"edge"`, `"shadow"`) in the JSON schema. The seven `game-art/specs/trevana-world` specs use them and were re-exported. Tests: `AutotileEdgeFinishTest`. Props: `NatureProps` adds `LEAFY_TREE`, `FRUIT_TREE`, `FLOWER_BUSH`, `MOSS_MOUND`, `BOULDER` and `RUIN_WALL` (outlined leaf-clump crowns, root-flared trunks, grass skirts, flat-shaded boulders, vine-covered ruins); the Trevana biome specs gained 28 of them, appended so no tile moved. Tests: `NaturePropsTest`. Cliffs and water have not been reworked yet.

* **Fix** - **The interface follows the screen: Auto interface size and a larger desktop window** ([accessibility](/client/accessibility.md)): the realm screen, HUD and F1 editors were drawn at one interface unit per physical pixel whatever the screen, so they were tiny on dense displays (a 3072 x 1920 laptop showed the realm list across a quarter of the width). `UiScales.AUTO` is now the default interface size; the desktop resolves it from the monitor's resolution and density (`UiScales.auto`: 175% on that panel, 200% on 4K, 100% on 1080p), and the window opens at 80% of the monitor (`DesktopWindow`). Manual sizes go up to 300%; saved sizes are kept. Android and the browser keep 100% for Auto. Tests in `AccessibilitySettingsTest` and `DesktopWindowTest`.

* **Add** - **Per-million drop chances** (`NpcDropEntry.chancePerMillion`, `RandomSource.rollPerMillion`): drops rarer than one basis point (1 in 10,000) can now be written in parts per million (400 = 1 in 2,500, 20 = 1 in 50,000); the NPC editor accepts `20ppm`, the bestiary and game wiki show "1 in N". Basis-point drops roll as before, so existing replays are unchanged. Trevana Realms: Starshards 1 in 2,500 and Wyrm Pearls 1 in 50,000 on every non-guaranteed drop.
