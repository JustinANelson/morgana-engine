# Page Events, Dialogue, and Quests

**Events** (`EventDefinition`) sit on a map tile (or are attached to an NPC via `eventId`) and contain ordered `EventPage`s.
- Active page: pages are evaluated from the **highest index to lowest**; the first whose `EventPageCondition` matches wins (switch, variable minimum, quest status, held item, minimum level).
- Triggers: `ACTION_INTERACT` (facing/current tile via interact), `PLAYER_TOUCH` (stepping on the tile), and `AUTORUN_ON_ENTER`.
- **Map-entry (autorun) events**: `fireMapEntryEvents` runs the active `AUTORUN_ON_ENTER` page of every event on the player's map (event-id order) after login, border crossing, warp tile, event `warp_player`, and warp spell. Admin teleports and death respawns do not fire them. Nested entries (an autorun page that warps into another autorun map) stop at depth 3, so warp loops terminate.
- Switches and variables have a `VariableScope`: **per character** (`PLAYER`, default: `CharacterState.switches/variables/stringVariables`) or **whole-world** (`WORLD`: `WorldSimulation.worldSwitches/worldVariables/worldStringVariables`, persisted across server restarts in `ServerState`). `EventPageCondition` (`switchScope`, `variableScope`), `EventCondition` (`scope`), `set_switch`, `set_variable`, `add_variable`, and `text_input` each carry a scope.

**Commands** (`EventCommand`, JSON `_type`): `dialogue` (with branching choices), `give_item`, `take_item`, `give_xp`, `set_switch`, `add_variable`, `start_quest`,
`complete_quest` (a repeatable quest with `cooldownTicks` stamps `PlayerQuestState.completedAtTick` and counts as `NOT_STARTED` again once that many world ticks have passed, so it is the basis of daily and weekly quests; `DailyQuestTest`), `warp_player`, `heal_player`, `open_shop`, `open_bank`, `open_crafting`, `play_animation`, `kotlin_hook`, and since 2026-11-13 `if`, `stop`, `set_variable`, `message` (a chat line, no window), `teach_spell`, `upgrade_gear` (`UpgradeGear(kind, slot, materialItemId, amount, successPercent)`, see [combat](/systems/combat.md)), `rebirth` (`Rebirth(classId)`, see [combat](/systems/combat.md)), `repair_gear` (`RepairGear(currencyItemId)`, repairs all worn and carried gear for a price or nothing), `run_common_event` (`RunCommonEvent(commonEventId)`), `wait` (`Wait(ticks)`, `1..12000`, pauses via `PendingEventWait` and resumes in `tickEventWaits`), `play_sound` (`PlaySound(soundId)` -> `s_play_sound`), `show_picture` (`ShowPicture(pictureId, title, durationTicks)` -> `s_show_picture`), `move_npc` (`MoveNpc(npcId, direction, steps, faceOnly)` moves/turns the nearest live NPC of that definition on the player's map and updates its `homeX`/`homeY`), `text_input` (`TextInput(prompt, variableId, scope, isNumeric, defaultValue, maxLength)` pauses via `PendingTextInput` / `s_text_input`, stores string or parsed integer into `stringVariables`/`variables` on `c_text_input`, and cancels remaining steps if dismissed), and flow labels/loops: `label` (`Label(name)`), `jump_to_label` (`JumpToLabel(name)` jumping forward or backward via `findLabelContinuation` within the active event or common event scope), `loop` (`Loop(commands)` repeating its body until `BreakLoop`, `Stop`, or `MAX_EVENT_STEPS`), and `break_loop` (`BreakLoop`, unwinding the innermost enclosing `Loop`).
**Text placeholders** (`formatEventText`): `dialogue` (speaker, text, and choice labels), `message` text, and `text_input` prompts/defaults expand `{player}`/`{name}`, `{level}`, `{map}`, `{visits}` (looks up player/world string then integer variables, defaulting to `0`), `{var:k}`, `{world:k}`, `{switch:k}`, `{world_switch:k}`, `{text:k}`, `{world_text:k}`, `{item:id}` (count in bag), `{item_name:id}`, `{vital:id}`, `{max_vital:id}`, and `{attr:id}`.
**Common events** (`CommonEventDefinition` in `common_events/`): reusable step sequences validated under `ContentCollection.COMMON_EVENTS` (same reference, depth `<= 8`, `ON_TIMER` `intervalTicks` in `1..72000`, and blank-name rules). Each carries a `CommonEventTrigger` (`MANUAL` by default, `ON_TIMER` ticking every `intervalTicks` for each online player in `tickTimedCommonEvents`, `ON_LOGIN` fired on new player spawns in `spawnOrUpdatePlayer`, or `ON_LEVEL_UP` fired in `grantXpToCharacter` when `levelsGained > 0`, bounded at depth 4 by `MAX_COMMON_EVENT_DEPTH`). `run_common_event` pushes the called common event's steps inline onto the active stack, so dialogues inside a common event pause and resume back into the caller, `stop` ends the entire run, and circular calls terminate cleanly at `MAX_EVENT_STEPS` (2000).
`kotlin_hook` emits `CustomHookRequested`, which the server forwards to plugins ([hook API](/server/hook_api.md)). `open_crafting` records a [crafting anchor](/systems/economy.md). Add a command: [playbook](/playbooks/add_event_command.md).

**Quests** (`QuestDefinition`): objectives of type `KILL_NPC`, `COLLECT_ITEM`, `TALK_TO_NPC`, `HARVEST_RESOURCE`, `CRAFT_RECIPE`, `VISIT_MAP` with a `targetId`
and `requiredCount`; per-player progress in `PlayerQuestState`. Completion requires all objectives; rewards are XP, currency, items, optionally a taught spell. Repeatable flag and prerequisites are supported.

**If steps** (`if`, `EventCommand.Conditional`): one primary `condition` plus optional `extraConditions: List<EventCondition>` combined via `matchMode` (`ConditionMatchMode.ALL` or `ConditionMatchMode.ANY`), and two step lists, `then` and `otherwise`; the event carries on with the next step afterwards. `EventCondition.type` chooses the check and which of its other fields matter: `SWITCH` (`key`; `value` 0 means "is off"), `VARIABLE` (`key`, `comparison`, `value`), `HAS_ITEM` (`targetId`, at least `value`), `QUEST` (`targetId`, `quest`: `NOT_STARTED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `READY_TO_COMPLETE` meaning every objective is met, or `COMPLETED`), `LEVEL` (`comparison`, `value`), `CLASS` and `KNOWS_SPELL` (`targetId`), `GUILD_HOLDS_AREA` (`value` 1: the player's guild holds the guild area; 0: it does not), and `CHANCE` (`value` in 100, drawn from the simulation's seeded generator so [replays](/systems/deterministic_replay.md) agree). Comparisons: `EQUAL`, `NOT_EQUAL`, `AT_LEAST`, `AT_MOST`, `GREATER`, `LESS`. Example: `{"_type":"if","condition":{"type":"HAS_ITEM","targetId":3,"value":2},"then":[...],"otherwise":[...]}`. Answers and branches may nest eight deep (`too_deep` beyond that), and one run stops after 2000 steps.

**Dialogues pause the event** (`runEventSteps`, `PendingDialogue`; changed 2026-11-13). A `dialogue` that offers answers, or that has steps after it, stops the run and remembers what is left (`WorldSimulation.pendingDialogues`, per player, transient like crafting anchors). The player's answer runs that answer's steps and then the rest of the event, so **answers work at any depth** and steps after a question run after it is answered. Before this, every step ran at once (several lines replaced one another on screen, and steps after a question ran before the answer) and only the answers of the page's *first* top-level dialogue did anything. A line with no answers continues when the client reports it closed: `c_dialogue_choice` with a choice index of `-1`, sent by clients that advertise the `dialogue_flow` capability (the game client; closing by the button, the X or a map change all count). For any other client (older, scripted, or nobody connected) the server continues at once, which is the old behaviour. On disconnect (`WorldSimulation.removePlayer` via `finishPendingEventsOnDisconnect`), an unanswered choice dialogue or `text_input` is discarded without guessing input, whereas a closing monologue (`choices.isEmpty()`) or pending `Wait` flushes its remaining non-interactive steps (ignoring waits/non-choice dialogues and stopping at any choice dialogue or `text_input`) before `MorganaWorldCoordinator.removeFromWorld` saves the character; a newer dialogue replaces whatever was waiting; and an answer that arrives with nothing waiting falls back to the page's first dialogue, so recordings made before this change replay the same.

Dialogue choices are answered with `c_dialogue_choice` ([client packets](/protocol/client_packets.md)). Admins with `EDIT_CONTENT` can test unsaved steps from any step in the [F1 editor](/client/f1_editor.md) via `testEventCommandsImpl` (`c_admin_test_event`), which validates references and runs the slice for the editing admin without altering the saved catalog. Validation of command references: [validation rules](/content/validation_rules.md).

**Arena queue** (`arena_queue`, `EventCommand.ArenaQueue`): runs `/arena join [size]`, `leave`, `rating` or `top` for the player, so an NPC can offer the [arena](/systems/arena.md) from a menu.

**Reset attributes** (`respec_attributes`, `EventCommand.RespecAttributes`, `RespecSystem.kt`, `RespecTest`): takes back every attribute point the player has spent (attributes above the class's starting values plus its free growth) as unspent points, for `pricePerPoint` of `currencyItemId` each (0 is free); nothing happens if they cannot pay or have nothing spent.
