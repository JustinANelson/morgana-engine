# Combat, Status Effects, Death, and Loot

## Pluggable Combat Architecture (`CombatModule`)
Combat mechanics in Morgana ORPG are completely decoupled from simulation orchestration via the pure domain `CombatModule` interface (`core-domain/src/main/kotlin/com/morgana/domain/combat/CombatModule.kt`):
- **`RealtimeCombatModule`** (id `realtime`; `morgana_classic` and `classic` still work as older names): The default native Kotlin combat implementation enforcing real-time tick-based combat. An unknown module id is an error (validator and `CombatModule.forId`), never a silent fallback.
- **Rules as data**: `combat.defaultAttackSpeedTicks` (12), `defaultNpcRespawnTicks` (100), `lootOwnerProtectionTicks` (600), `groundItemDespawnTicks` (2400) and `partyXpBonusPercent` (20) are fields of the game's `combat` definition; the values in parentheses are the defaults, which equal the old hard-coded numbers (`CombatRulesAsDataTest`).
- **Monster pacing as data**: `combat.npcs` (`maxAttackersPerTarget`, `firstStrikeDelayTicks`, `spellWindUpTicks`, `casterKeepsDistance`; all off by default) shapes how packs and casters fight; see [NPC AI](/systems/npc_ai.md) (`NpcPacingTest`).
- **`LuaCombatModule`**: A script-driven adapter delegating combat hooks (`on_attack`, `on_cast`, `on_tick_projectiles`, `on_tick_players`, `handle_action`) to sandboxed Lua scripts via the `world` and `register.combat` APIs.
- **`NoOpCombatModule`**: A peaceful/non-combat module for peaceful exploration worlds, narrative games, or gathering simulations.

## Action-combat content (not executed yet)
Skills timed in ticks and combo graphs exist as content (`action_skills`, `weapon_sets`) and are run by the fourth module, `action` ([action combat](/systems/action_combat.md), built 2026-11-21, which wraps the realtime module); none of the three modules above reads them. Format and rules: [action-combat content](/domain/action_combat_content.md).

## Generic Action Routing (`c_action`)
In addition to legacy attack and cast messages, clients can dispatch generic actions via `ClientPacket.CustomAction(actionId, params)` (`c_action`), recorded in the deterministic replay log as `SimInput.CustomAction`.
- The simulation forwards custom actions first to `combat.handleAction(sim, charId, actionId, params)`.
- If unhandled by the combat module, it executes script-registered actions from `scripts.registry.executeAction(...)`.

## Real-time combat mechanics
**Attack** (`playerAttack`): cooldown is the equipped weapon's `attackSpeedTicks` (`combat.defaultAttackSpeedTicks` in `game.json` when the weapon sets none; 12 by default), tracked via `lastAttackTick`.
- Ranged weapons consume ammo and spawn a server-tracked projectile (range, speed, collision).
- Melee/staff hits the facing tile(s) up to weapon range: NPCs first, then players if the map's moral allows PvP.
- Facing a resource node with a tool swings at it as a harvest ([interaction](/systems/inventory_interaction.md)).
- Crit roll: `rollBasisPoints(critChance)`; formulas in [stats and formulas](/domain/stats_and_formulas.md).
- **Weapon Proficiency** (`game.weaponProficiency`, optional): when enabled, weapons with `ItemDefinition.weaponType` gain hit progress on landed melee, bow projectile, and weapon-skill (`baseDamagePercent > 0`) hits (`hitsPerLevel * currentLevel` per level up to `maxLevel`), scaling attack and weapon-skill damage by `+damageBonusPercentPerLevel` per level above 1 and gating spells with `weaponTypeReqs` / `weaponProficiencyLevelReq`.

**Spells** (`playerCastSpell`): checks class/level requirements, vital costs, and per-spell cooldown.
- **Cast times & Channeling**: when `spell.castTimeTicks > 0`, the player enters casting state (`CharacterState.isCasting`, `castingSpellId`), vital costs are spent upfront, and `SpellCastStarted` is emitted. A moving player interrupts casting immediately (`SpellCastInterrupted`, cleared state).
- **Overhead Cast Bar**: clients render an overhead cyan progress bar with the spell name above the caster's HP bar while casting.
- **Resolution**: when `currentTick >= castFinishTick` in `tickPlayers`, casting state is cleared, the spell cooldown is set, `SpellCast` is emitted, and the spell effect resolves.
- **Target types**: self, single target, directional projectile (with optional `pierce` to pass through lined-up targets), AoE self/target-centered, ground-targeted AoE (`GROUND_AOE`, centered on the aimed tile clamped to `rangeTiles`, supporting both damage and ally healing), and warp. Spells may apply timed status effects.

**Status effects** (`StatusEffectType`): `DOT_POISON`, `DOT_BURN`, `DOT_BLEED`, `HOT_REGEN`, `STUN`, `ROOT`, `TAUNT` (a monster fights whoever applied it while it lasts: `NpcAiSystem` forces the target, ignored if the taunter is dead or on another map), `SLOW` (on monsters it stretches walking and striking time by its magnitude in percent, `NpcAiSystem`), `HASTE`, `SHIELD`, `STAT_BUFF`, `STAT_DEBUFF`; ticked every tick on players and NPCs, stun/root block movement.

**Regeneration**: natural regen every 40 ticks (2 s). `HEAL_TRAP` tiles fire when `currentTick % intervalTicks == 0`.

**NPC death**: respawn timer from the spawn slot; all entries in the NPC's multi-drop loot table are evaluated independently (`chanceBasisPoints`, amount range) onto the ground; XP awarded. Spawn variants (`EnemyVariantDefinition`) raise the monster's effective level (`effectiveLevel(npcDef.level) = baseLevel + levelBonus`) for nameplate tier colors, level-offset XP scaling, and dropped item level rolls. In a game with `loot` rules each dropped piece of gear lies as its own item with a rolled rarity and affixes at the monster's effective level, and worn rolled damage adds to the weapon's ([loot rarities and affixes](/systems/loot_and_affixes.md)).
**XP sharing**: with a party that shares XP and 2+ living members on the killer's map, total = base x1.20, split evenly (min 1); otherwise solo.
Kill objectives advance quests ([events and quests](/systems/events_quests.md)).

**Player death & PK rules**: on `OPEN_PVP` maps all non-bound inventory items drop to the ground (despawn after 2400 ticks = 2 min). When `combat.pk` (`PkRulesDefinition`) is configured, players also use `PkMode` (`PEACE`, `TEAM`, `GUILD`, `PK`, toggled via `ClientPacket.SetPkMode` / `SimInput.SetPkMode`) to filter PvP targeting in `WorldSimulation.canAttackPlayer`; killing an innocent player adds `pointsPerKill`, Red Name (`redNameThreshold`) / Black Name (`blackNameThreshold`) players risk dropping equipped non-bound gear (`redEquipDropChanceBp` / `blackEquipDropChanceBp`) and respawn in `jailMapId` (when set), `GUARD` NPCs auto-aggro rogue/Red/Black players, and `pkPoints` decay by 1 every `decayIntervalTicks` while online. Otherwise respawn at the class start map/tile, or in place on `ARENA` maps ([maps and tiles](/domain/maps_and_tiles.md)).

Floating combat text categories include `DAMAGE` and `CRIT_DAMAGE` ([server packets](/protocol/server_packets.md)).

**Ally support spells**: a `SELF`-targeted spell with `allyTargetable = true` resolves through `pickAllySpellTarget`: the explicit `targetEntityId` when it is a living party/guild ally within `rangeTiles`, otherwise (healing only) the most wounded such ally, otherwise the caster. The client does not yet send ally targets, so in practice healers auto-target. Conquer Online's `Cure` and `Stigma` use this; `Revive` is a true resurrection through the ghost state below.

**Gear durability**: when `durability` (`DurabilityDefinition`) is set, `registerWeaponHit` wears the equipped weapon after each landed hit and `wearArmor` wears one random worn armor piece whenever a player takes damage, each by a percent chance drawn from the seeded `rng`. `ItemStack.durability` runs 100 to 0; at 0 the piece stays equipped but `EquipmentLoadout.active` / `getActive` skip it, so it adds no attributes, defense, damage or weapon stats. The `repair_gear` event step restores everything worn and carried for `repairPricePercent` of each item's price, or nothing if the player cannot pay for all of it.

**Rebirth**: when `rebirth` (`RebirthDefinition`) is set, the `rebirth` event step runs `rebirthCharacter`: a character at `minLevel` or above (and under `maxRebirths`) restarts at `resetLevel`, optionally as another class, with attributes rebuilt from that class (base plus auto growth for the levels regained), `bonusStatPointsPerRebirth` per rebirth held as unspent points, only the spells the class can use plus its starting and rebirth-granted spells, gear that no longer fits moved to the bag, and the count stored in the player variable `countVariable` (`{rebirths}` in event text). Spells with `rebirthReq` need that many rebirths to cast, learn from a book, or receive from `teach_spell`.

**Paragon**: when `paragon` (`ParagonDefinition`) is set, XP earned at the level cap fills a track instead of being dropped: every `xpBase + xpStep x paragonLevel` XP gives a paragon level (kept in the player variable `paragon.variable`, shown as `{paragon}`) and `statPointsPerLevel` unspent attribute points, up to `maxLevels`. `CharacterState.gainExperience` does it, `CompiledGame.xpToNext(character)` gives the bar's cost, and the character sheet shows `Paragon N`. Nothing resets, so it is the non-destructive alternative to rebirth. Tests: `ParagonTest`.

**Gems and upgrades**: `ItemStack.sockets` holds one entry per socket (0 empty, else a gem item id). A gem item has `ItemDefinition.gem` (`GemEffect`); `EquipmentLoadout` adds its attribute bonuses, damage and armour for every unbroken worn piece, and `totalGemXpPercent` scales kill XP in `awardPartyOrSoloExperience`. The `upgrade_gear` event step (`GearUpgradeSystem.upgradeGear`) opens sockets (`gearUpgrade.maxSockets`), sets gems, moves gear to `nextTierItemId` (needs the player's level) or re-rolls it at the next `loot.rarities` entry via `ItemRoller.rollAtRarity`; every check happens before materials are taken, a failed chance roll still consumes them, and `+` level, sockets and durability are kept.

**Chain upgrade costs**: `ItemDefinition.upgradeCost` (`UpgradeCost`: `materials` item id to amount, and `gold` of the game's currency item) is what upgrading that piece to its `nextTierItemId` costs. When the `upgrade_gear` level upgrade names no `materialItemId` and the worn item has one, all of it is charged together after the level check. Test: `ChainUpgradeTest`.

**Starting sockets**: `ItemDefinition.sockets` (0 to 6) is how many empty sockets a new piece of that gear starts with; `addItem`, monster drops and generated floor loot all use `ItemDefinition.freshSockets()`, which is empty for anything that stacks. Trevana Realms sets it on every piece (Weapon 2, Chest 2, others 1) and has no way to add sockets. Test: `StartingSocketsTest`.

**Quality raises with pity** (`gearUpgrade.quality`, `QualityUpgradeDefinition`, `QualityStep`): when set, `UPGRADE_QUALITY` takes its chance and cost from the step for the rarity it reaches (the event only supplies the material and the slot). A step under 10000 basis points can fail: the pearls are used up, the failure adds `pityGainPerFailure` stacks (up to `maxPityStacks`, held on `CharacterState.upgradePityStacks`), and each stack adds `pityBonusBasisPointsPerStack` to the next chancy try; a chancy success spends all stacks. A success also rolls `socketChanceBasisPoints` for one new empty socket while the gear has room (`maxSockets`); that roll and `ADD_SOCKET` are the only ways to get sockets besides dropped loot. Tests: `QualityUpgradeTest`.

**Enhancement ladder** (`gearUpgrade.enhancement`, `EnhancementDefinition`, the `ENHANCE` kind): an optional alternative to merging for games that want a Black Desert style `+` ladder. Each `EnhancementTier` names its material, amount and chance; tiers at 10000 never fail and build no pity, lower tiers share the same `upgradePityStacks`, and a failure takes `failDurabilityLoss` durability and, from `dropPlusOnFailFrom`, one `+`. When a game sets `enhancement`, `mergeGear` is closed. Trevana Realms does not use it: its `+` levels come from merging. Tests: `EnhancementTest`.

**XP gauge**: when `xpGauge` (`XpGaugeDefinition`) is set, `chargeXpGauge` runs after every NPC kill by a player: it adds `gainPerKill` to the gauge vital (capped at its max; passive fill is that vital's own `regen`) and adds `SpellDefinition.killExtendTicks` to the remaining time of each of the killer's running `spell-<id>` effects. XP skills need no special cast path: they list the gauge vital in `costs`, so the usual vital-cost check spends it. Conquer Online's Cyclone and Superman use this. The HUD draws the gauge's bar only for a character whose class can cast a skill that spends it (`VitalBars`); every other vital always shows.

**Class promotions**: when `promotion` (`PromotionDefinition`) is set, a character holds a tier number in the player variable `promotion.variable` (0 = `startName`). The `promote` event step runs `promoteCharacter`: it needs the next tier's `minLevel`, then raises the variable by one, adds `bonusStatPoints` and teaches the tier's `grantSpellIds` that the class can use; it never takes a price, so events charge items first (`take_item` inside a `has_item` check). `promotionReq` on a spell blocks casting (`playerCastSpellImpl`), learning from a book and `teach_spell`; on an item it blocks wearing or using it (`useInventorySlotImpl`) and an `UPGRADE_LEVEL` into a tier the character may not wear. Without a `promotion` section any `promotionReq` above 0 is never met. Each tier can also carry per-class data: `classNames` (a class's own title for the tier, used by `{promotion}`, `{rank}` and the promotion notices through `PromotionDefinition.nameOf(tier, classId)`) and `classGrantSpellIds` (spells taught only to that class, on top of `grantSpellIds`). Content validation checks that the classes and spells they name exist. Rebirth keeps the tier.

**Fly and Reflect**: two status effects in `ReflectAndFly.kt`. `FLY` (`CharacterState.isFlying`) makes `movePlayerImpl` accept any in-bounds tile, except on the `guildWar` map (`mayFlyOver`), and melee cannot reach the flier: `ATTACK_ON_SIGHT` monsters in `NpcAiSystem` and the player melee target search in `playerAttackImpl` skip them, while projectiles and spells still land. `REFLECT` (`reflectPercent`, the strongest running effect) returns that percent of a melee hit to the attacker: after an NPC melee hit (`reflectOntoNpc`, which can kill the monster and credits the defender) and in `applyPvpDamage` (`reflectOntoPlayer`, which never takes the attacker below 1 life).

**Ghost state**: when `ghost` (`GhostDefinition`) is set, `handlePlayerDeath` still drops items and clears effects but leaves the player in place with `isGhost = true`, life 0 and `ghostSinceTick`; arena (`safeRespawnInPlace`) and PK-jail deaths respawn at once as before. Because life is 0, every `isAlive` check refuses a ghost's attacks, casts, item use, pickups and shop actions, and monsters and projectiles skip them; only `movePlayerImpl` and the `/release` command (handled in `AdminSystem`) accept a ghost. A `SELF` spell with `revivePercent > 0` revives the named or nearest ghost within `rangeTiles` (`reviveGhost`); `releaseGhostImpl` sends a ghost to their class start with full vitals, run by `/release`, by `autoReleaseTicks` in `tickPlayers`, and when a ghost logs in. The client draws ghosts with a pale translucent tint via `PublicPlayerSnapshot.isGhost`.

**Death cost and `/revive`**: `GhostDefinition.deathWearPoints` takes durability off every worn piece in `handlePlayerDeath` (needs `durability`; the death notice states the amount and the `/revive` wait). `selfReviveAfterTicks > 0` enables `/revive` (`selfReviveImpl`, routed in `AdminSystem` beside `/release`): after the wait the ghost stands up where they fell with `selfRevivePercent` of their life.

**Rested XP and catch-up**: `rested` (`RestedDefinition`) applies to monster kills only, via `withRestedXp` in both the solo and party XP paths. `tickPlayers` stamps `lastSeenWorldTick` every 200 ticks; on login `withRestedPoolFromAbsence` adds `poolPercentPerOfflineHour` of the level's XP per hour away to `restedXp`, capped at `poolCapPercent`. A kill adds up to `bonusPercent` of its XP from the pool (spending it) and `catchUpPercent` while the level is under `catchUpBelowLevel`. Nothing applies at the level cap.

**Dual-wielding**: when `dualWield` (`DualWieldDefinition`) is set and the character's class is allowed (`classIds`, empty = all), using a one-handed melee weapon (`ItemDefinition.canDualWield`) while a one-handed weapon is already in the main hand and the `SHIELD` slot is empty wears it in the off-hand instead (`wantsOffHand`); otherwise a weapon swaps the main hand as before, and a two-handed weapon or a shield bumps the off-hand weapon to the bag. After a landed melee hit on an NPC or player, `rollOffHandStrike` draws `strikeChancePercent` from the seeded RNG (only when a usable off-hand weapon is worn, so other games replay unchanged) and returns a non-critical hit made with the off-hand weapon (its damage, `+` level, rolls and gems) scaled by `damagePercent` and that weapon's proficiency; the target is re-read first so a kill by the main hand is not hit twice. `EquipmentLoadout.rolledAttackDamage` skips the off-hand weapon so the main swing is unchanged. Off-hand strikes do not wear durability or train proficiency. Defaults (65% x 80%) average about half a weapon of extra damage per swing, which beats a shield's few points of defence.

**Tank and support roles**: `SpellDefinition.groupTarget` (SELF spells only) lands the spell on the caster and every living party or guild ally within `rangeTiles`, in id order, so a heal or buff reaches the whole group; `SpellDefinition.cleanses` (SELF spells only) strips every status with `isNegative` from the recipient before the spell's own status is applied, and with `allyTargetable` and no named target `pickAllySpellTarget` picks the ally with the most harmful statuses. A taunt is an `AOE_SELF_CENTERED` spell with `statusEffectType = TAUNT`. Both fields are checked by the content validator. Tests: `GroupRolesTest`.

**Threat table** (`combat.npcs.threatSwitchPercent`, `ThreatSystem` in `systems/ThreatSystem.kt`, a `MonsterBehavior`, `ThreatTest`): off by default (a monster turns on whoever hit it last). With a value of 100 or more every hit adds its damage to the attacker's threat on that monster (`MapNpcInstance.threat`), healing an ally the monster is fighting adds `healThreatPercent` of the amount to the healer's threat on it, and a taunt lifts the taunter's threat to `tauntThreatPercent` of the highest. A monster keeps its target until someone's threat exceeds the target's by the switch percent; players who died or left the map are ignored; the table clears on death, leash and reset.

Pets fight beside their owner: see [Pets](/systems/pets.md).
